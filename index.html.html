5-Point Fraud Prevention Checklist

Must-read safety rules before communicating with any packing supplier online.

1
NEVER Pay Advance Money: Genuine suppliers never demand registration fees, file charges, security deposits, or gate passes before delivering work.
2
Verify GST & Physical Office: Ask for valid GSTIN registration, company trade license, and physical warehouse address in your state.
3
Demand Written Agreement: Insist on written documentation detailing per-box piece rates, payment schedules, and defective item rules.
4
Inspect Sample Batches Locally: Whenever possible, inspect initial raw material samples and confirm pickup/delivery transport arrangements.
5
Beware of Fake Brand Authorization: No major corporate brand (Natraj, Apsara, DOMS, Classmate, etc.) offers direct 'work-from-home salary contracts' on Telegram/WhatsApp. Work is managed independently by third-party local distributors.
